HighScore

Parameters

Set these query parameters in the page URL to configure the game.

  • mode=standard or mode=debug
  • high_contrast=true
  • colorscheme=output, high_contrast, pop_is_everything, normal_colors, 70, or Gasoline%20Rainbow
  • sounds=on or sounds=off
  • show_fps=true or show_fps=false
  • draw_constraints=true or draw_constraints=false
